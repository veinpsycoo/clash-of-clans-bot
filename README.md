## Features

*   **Auto farm:** Reads opponent loot, filters by minimum loot requirements, and attacks using the army in the bottom bar day and night.
*   **Smart attack:** Locates Air Defenses, triggers hero abilities at the right time, and spreads troops across 9 deployment points.
*   **Wall upgrades:** Spends full storages on walls with gold and elixir; wall mode reserves one builder specifically for walls.
*   **Auto upgrades:** Initiates building and hero upgrades recommended by the game and verifies spending.
*   **Laboratory:** Starts research automatically so the queue never sits empty.
*   **Clan and rewards:** Requests Clan Castle reinforcements and collects achievements and rewards.
*   **Telegram control:** Manage the bot remotely with `/durum`, `/baslat`, and `/durdur` commands plus hourly summaries.
*   **Multiple accounts:** Cycles through saved accounts on the device sequentially.
*   **Exit at percent:** Leaves battles when destruction hits your target percentage; supports separate minimums for gold, elixir, and dark elixir.
*   **On-screen log:** Displays latest actions, loot gained per attack, and session summary directly over the game interface.
*   **In-app updates:** Prompts to download new versions automatically while keeping settings and license intact.
*   **Gems: only with approval:** Gem spending and speed-ups occur only if you manually tick the checkbox; it never spends real money.
*   **Two-corner attacks:** Drops troops on left and right corners alternately, changing sides and points irregularly like a human player.
*   **All spells:** Casts Lightning on Air Defenses and support spells (Rage, Freeze, Invisibility) until the spell card is empty.
*   **Loot chart:** Visualizes gold, elixir, and dark elixir gains from every attack within the app.
*   **In-game button:** A small overlay button allows one-tap start/stop; long-press for screen test.
*   **Interface in 9 languages:** Supports English, Turkish, Spanish, Portuguese, Russian, Arabic, Indonesian, German, and French.

## How it works

1.  **Scan:** The bot reads the opponent's loot and checks bases individually to avoid wasting time.
2.  **Filter:** It skips any village that does not meet the minimum loot filter criteria and uses available troops from the bottom bar.
3.  **Spread:** The army is distributed around the village at 9 specific points.
4.  **Strike:** It identifies Air Defenses and drops 3 Lightning Spells on each. It utilizes Clan Castle lightning, Siege Machines, and hero abilities at optimal moments.
5.  **Spend:** Full storages are spent on walls, building upgrades, hero upgrades, and Laboratory research, with verification of spending.

## Telegram control

Enter your bot token in the PC panel, then control cocbot remotely from Telegram:

*   `/durum` — status (running, mode, last attack)
*   `/baslat` — start
*   `/durdur` — stop

An hourly summary arrives automatically.

## Safety settings

*   **Gems only with your approval:** gem spending and speed-ups work only through a checkbox you tick yourself — it never spends real money.
*   **Caps:** set per-action and daily gem limits, plus a storage percentage that decides when it attacks.
*   **Settings stick:** a box is saved the moment you tick it and applies from the next session.
*   **Safe stop:** Stop halts the bot at a safe point.
